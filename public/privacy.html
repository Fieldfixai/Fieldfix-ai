Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026
FieldFix AI LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — we never store your actual password)
  • Subscription plan and billing status
  • Account creation date

1.2 Usage Data

When you use the Service we collect:

  • Troubleshooting queries you submit
  • AI responses generated for your queries
  • Equipment categories selected
  • Number of queries used per day
  • Session timestamps and activity logs

1.3 Payment Information

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number, CVV, or other sensitive payment details. We only receive confirmation of successful payments from Stripe.

1.4 Technical Data

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and features used

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the FieldFix AI service
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send important account notifications
  • Respond to support requests
  • Monitor for abuse and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the AI responses
  • Send product updates (you can opt out anytime)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Anthropic — Your troubleshooting queries are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com
  • Stripe — Payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com
  • Supabase — Database hosting. Your data is stored securely in Supabase's infrastructure
  • Railway — Application hosting
  • Law enforcement — If required by law or to protect our rights

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Search history is retained for 90 days. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days except where required by law.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Passwords stored as secure cryptographic hashes
  • Session tokens with expiration
  • Account sharing prevention
  • Regular security monitoring

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out — Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights email hello@fieldfix.io.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. Your session token is stored in your browser's local storage.

8. Children's Privacy

FieldFix AI is designed for professional use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights contact hello@fieldfix.io.

10. International Users

FieldFix AI is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through the Service. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Website: fieldfix.io
  • Company: FieldFix AI LLC — Texas, United States
