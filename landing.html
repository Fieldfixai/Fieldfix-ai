Fanuc 0i-MD alarm SV0023 on X axis, machine won't move
⚙️
SV0023 — Overcurrent Alarm

Root causes (High → Low):
1. Servo amp overcurrent — check amp LED
2. Motor winding short — measure phases
3. Encoder cable damaged — check continuity

Step 1: SYSTEM → Servo Diagnostic — check DC bus (280-320VDC)
Step 2: Power off. Measure U-V-W phases — expect 2-8Ω equal
Step 3: Check encoder connector at motor for bent pins

Tools: Multimeter (DC 600V), ohmmeter
Est. repair: 30-90 min
